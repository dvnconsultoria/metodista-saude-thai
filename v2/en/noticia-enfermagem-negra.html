Exhibition in Salvador highlights the history of Black nursing

Source: Cofen · November 13, 2026

Cofen reported that an exhibition on Black nursing is scheduled for November 16–30, 2026, at Shopping Piedade in Salvador. Organized with Coren-BA and the Anna Nery National Nursing Museum, the exhibition presents historical records and professional journeys connected to nursing in Brazil.
